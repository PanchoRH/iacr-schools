About the program

Designed for learning, not just presenting.

A Cryptology School is typically full-time and open to qualified participants, with a small number of experts focused on teaching rather than showcasing results.

Focused topics

Each school develops depth around a clearly identified topic in cryptology and related fields.

Expert-led teaching

Lectures, tutorials and discussion emphasize understanding, durable skills and research capacity.

International reach

The program strengthens local research communities and connects participants with the wider IACR ecosystem.
