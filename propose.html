Proposal format

Required information

Name and topic

Describe the topic and explain its relevance to cryptology research.

Objective

State the goals, prerequisites, expected benefits and teaching materials.

Attendance

Estimate the number of participants.

Location and dates

Describe travel, housing, local transport, venue arrangements and accessibility.

Speakers

List potential speakers and identify confirmed or soft-confirmed speakers.

Teaching format

Describe the schedule, lecture length and session format.

Budget

Give a detailed budget, other funding sources and responsibility for any deficit. The school should not make a profit.

Evaluation

Review criteria

Topic

Relevance to cryptology and future research.

Impact

Value for education and research.

Team

Quality of speakers and credibility of organizers.

Plan

Feasible organization and budget.
