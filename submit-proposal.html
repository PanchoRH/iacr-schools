Online submission

Send your proposal

The official IACR policy (PDF) specifies the proposal format and evaluation criteria. Give short answers below and include the full details in your proposal PDF. All fields are required unless marked optional.

No account is needed. Your documents will be included as email attachments to the Schools Committee.

1. Organizing team
Names, affiliations and roles of the other organizers. If you are the sole organizer, say so.
2. School details
Who is the school for, what should participants already know, and what will they learn?
3. Teaching, logistics and budget
Outline the schedule, lecture length, sessions and teaching materials.
Include local transport and accessibility arrangements.
Summarize income, expenses, other funding and responsibility for any deficit. Include the detailed budget in your documents.
4. Documents and confirmation

Attach one proposal PDF and up to four supporting documents. Maximum 5 MiB per file and 10 MiB altogether. For larger submissions, email schools@iacr.org.

    PDF, DOCX, XLSX, ODT, ODS, CSV or TXT. Choose all supporting files together; selecting again replaces the previous selection.

      Before you send

      What to include

      Proposal PDF

      Follow the proposal guide and include the teaching plan, speakers, dates, location and detailed budget.

      Supporting documents

      Attach any additional material, such as a budget spreadsheet, draft programme or letters of support. For large files, include a link the committee can access.

      School name and contact details

      Include the school name in the email subject and the organizing team’s names, affiliations and contact details in your message.
